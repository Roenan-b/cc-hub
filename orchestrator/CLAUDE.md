# Lead session workspace

This directory is the lead (orchestrator) session's working directory. The lead's full operating instructions are in `roles/lead.md`, which the hub appends to its system prompt, and the shared Obsidian vault is added with `--add-dir`.

Put anything here you want the lead to always know about you and your environment, for example:

- Where your repositories live and which ones matter most
- Standing preferences (report style, working hours, what to never touch)
- Accounts and services the team may or may not use

Keep personal details out of this file if you publish your fork.

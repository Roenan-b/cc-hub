# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want cc-hub to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, or identity. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to act with respect and good faith in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any community channel.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, and focus it on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work and own your mistakes.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic posting.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Roenan-b) or, for anything security related, a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Roenan-b/cc-hub/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful, depending on its severity and whether it is repeated.
